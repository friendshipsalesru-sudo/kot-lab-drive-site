KOT-LAB Drive

KOT-LAB Drive is a personal, non-commercial integration used by its owner to access and transfer files in the owner's Google Drive account using the open-source rclone application.

Google Drive access is authorized directly through Google's OAuth consent flow. This informational website does not sign in to Google, read Google Drive files, or collect passwords or OAuth tokens.

The owner may revoke the integration's access in Google Account settings at any time.
