Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

KOT-LAB Drive is a personal, non-commercial integration used by its owner with the open-source rclone application.

Google Drive access

With authorization from the account owner, rclone may access files, folders, and file metadata in the owner's Google Drive and may transfer or modify them within the permissions granted during OAuth authorization.

Purpose

The integration is used only for the owner's own requested file transfers, backups, synchronization, and related operations. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage

OAuth credentials and transferred files are handled by the owner's rclone installation and storage systems. This informational website does not receive or store Google Drive files or OAuth tokens. Operational logs may contain file names and transfer details and are managed on the owner's systems.

Sharing

Data may be transferred to storage destinations configured by the owner. The integration is not offered as a public multi-user service. Google Drive data is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Revocation

The owner can revoke account access at Google Account connections. Revoking Google access does not automatically delete local copies already transferred by rclone.

Website

This website has no login form, advertising, or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, may process connection information under its own policies.
